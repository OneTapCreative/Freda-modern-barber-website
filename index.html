Common questions

Before you
take the chair.

Do you accept walk-ins?+

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome when time is available, but booking ahead is the best way to secure your preferred appointment.

How early should I arrive?+

Please arrive five minutes before your appointment. This gives us enough time for a quick consultation without delaying the next client.

What booking platforms can this site use?+

The booking button can connect to Square Appointments, Booksy, Squire, TheCut, Fresha, Vagaro, or another scheduling service.

Can I reschedule my appointment?+

Yes. Rescheduling is available through the booking platform or by contacting the studio before the cancellation window.
